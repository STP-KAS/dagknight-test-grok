## Later the same day — Sutton on parallel DeFi

https://x.com/michaelsuttonil/status/2098204180406026482

Working hypothesis: global DeFi is not sequential; economic events are concurrent; global picture is aggregation + iterative convergence. Consensus still needs relative order on *related* sub-series (double spend). Designs where ~all mutations hit a constant number of sequential bottlenecks are the mistake.

Follow-up: if that bottleneck is mandatory, even well-designed ZK cannot scale — missing piece of the vProgs post. Quoted context: push apps to partitioned/parallel/replicated state; computational scalability is the more fundamental point; based ZK = L1 DA on every event.

This is the app-layer dual of DAGKnight (order the related sub-series at real latency; do not serialize the world). Logged in `docs/06-partitioned-defi.md`.
