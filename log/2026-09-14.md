## Extra intel: DAGKnight in Go (Hoosat, not Kaspa)

User asked to browse DAGKnight Go. There is a live Go implementation, and it is **not** Kaspa core.

- Repo: [HoosatNetwork/HTND](https://github.com/HoosatNetwork/HTND) (fork of `kaspanet/kaspad`), `ghostdagmanager` package. Branch/tag `DAGKnight`. Last seen pushing Sep 2026.
- Author claim (May 2026): public testnet running DAGKnight at ~5 BPS on the Go node. Separate coin, separate HF schedule.
- Their own faithfulness note (`domain/consensus/processes/ghostdagmanager/dagknight-faithfulness.md`, Feb 2026, self-rated ~90–92%) lists deviations vs eprint 2022/1494 that Kaspa's rusty-kaspa path does **not** copy:

| Paper | Hoosat Go | Why it is not a Kaspa pin |
|-------|-----------|---------------------------|
| `g(k) = ⌊√k⌋` | `g(k) = k` | More conservative, not the paper parameter |
| Recurse from `tips(G)` of the sub-DAG | Always global consensus tips | Engineering shortcut |
| `reps_G(P)` sampling | Iterate full `P` | Fine only while \|P\| is tiny |
| Anticone total order unspecified | Hash-string lexicographic | Deterministic proxy |
| k search 0,1,2,3,… | 0,1,2,4,6,… + backtrack ≥4 | Empirical, not paper |

Kaspa rusty-kaspa `dagknight` is dual-coloring + incremental UMC cascade + `ForkActivation` gating + TN13. Hoosat is a fork of the **retired Go kaspad**, running on its own network. Useful as an existence proof that a Go translation can mine a DAGKnight-shaped DAG; **not** a source of rank/UMC/tie-break patches for `kaspanet/rusty-kaspa`.

Do not mix Hoosat's `g(k)=k` into Kaspa PRs. Do not cite Hoosat BPS as Kaspa TN13 evidence.
